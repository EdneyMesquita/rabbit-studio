# Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-10.

Rabbit Studio is a desktop app that runs on your computer. It has no account and no telemetry, and the project runs no server that the app talks to. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

## What stays on your computer

- **Clusters:** the clusters you save, including names, addresses and settings, are stored on your computer. Passwords and passphrases go to your system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, or Secret Service on Linux).
- **Your data:** the app connects only to the RabbitMQ servers you set up. It reads and writes their queues, messages and definitions only when you ask it to: browsing a queue, peeking or moving a message, publishing, changing a setting.
- **Your files:** exports and definition files are saved only where you choose.
- **Log file:** the app writes a log of its own activity and errors to your computer, to help diagnose problems: app start and exit, cluster names and server hosts, failed or slow operations, and crashes. It never contains passwords, connection strings or message contents. It is never sent anywhere; you can open it from the app (Ctrl+K, "Open logs folder") and delete it at any time.
